My Story

A Strategist Who Delivers, Not Just Advises

I'm Muhammad Saad — a Flutter and AI Developer based in Gujranwala, Pakistan, with a BS in Software Engineering from the University of Gujrat. I build cross-platform mobile applications for Android & iOS using Flutter and Dart, with strong expertise in state management (Provider & Riverpod), Firebase, and RESTful API integration.

My work sits at the intersection of data and human behaviour. I believe that great software is the kind users never have to think about — fast, reliable, and it just works. I don't take on projects I can't deliver results on. If your goals are realistic and your commitment is genuine, I bring the same level of commitment to your project as I would to my own brand.

Beyond mobile, I build LLM-powered agents with LangChain.js and LangGraph.js, including RepoPilot, a multi-agent codebase onboarding assistant. My code is structured to last and built for real-world scale. When I'm not coding, you'll find me playing cricket or writing poetry.
