Privacy Policy

Gmail Job Agent · Last updated October 3, 2026

About this app

Gmail Job Agent is a personal tool built and operated by Muhammad Saad. It reads job-application and interview emails in the developer's own Gmail account and organizes the key details (company, role, status and dates) into an Excel spreadsheet. It is not offered to the public, and no one other than the developer is asked to sign in.

Data the app accesses

The app requests read-only access to Gmail through Google's OAuth. It uses this access only to find and read emails related to job applications and interviews. It cannot send, delete or modify emails.

  • Email content such as the sender, subject, date and body of job-related messages.
  • The OAuth token that keeps the app signed in, stored locally on the developer's own computer.

How the data is used

Email content is used for one purpose: extracting structured job-application details into a spreadsheet. To do this, the text of relevant emails is sent to a third-party AI model provider for processing. The data is not used for advertising, profiling, or training any model by the developer, and it is not sold or shared with anyone else.

Storage and retention

Extracted results are saved in an Excel file on the developer's own device. The app does not run a public server or database, and no copy of your Gmail data is kept elsewhere. You can delete the spreadsheet and stored token at any time.

Google API Services policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can remove the app's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
